Chicken Siomai

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ground chicken
  • 1/2 pc onion, minced
  • 4 tbsp spring onions, chopped
  • 1/4 cup carrots, minced
  • 1/4 cup singkamas, finely minced
  • 3 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 pc egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp five spice powder (optional)
  • wonton wrappers, as needed
  • chopped spring onions and carrots for topping

Method

  1. Combine ground chicken and minced shrimp in a bowl. Gradually mix together.
  2. Add carrot, onion, scallion, egg, ground black pepper, and sesame oil. Mix until well blended.
  3. Add Knorr SavorRich Liquid Seasoning. Mix well.
  4. Scoop 1 1/2 tablespoons of the mixture and wrap in wonton wrapper. Repeat until all mixture is used.
  5. Arrange in a steamer and steam for 15–20 minutes.
  6. Transfer to a serving plate. Serve with toyo, calamansi, and chili garlic paste.
